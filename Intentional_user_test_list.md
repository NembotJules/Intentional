# Intentional: test it like a normal user

Use the app as if you'd just downloaded it. For each line, do the thing in **bold**, then check you see what follows the arrow (→). If something's off, note the number and what you saw.

Tip: some checks need time to pass, such as a habit's start time going by or a Sunday. Those are marked ⏳; do them when the moment comes.

---

## A. First launch (onboarding)
1. **Settings → Replay onboarding** (or a fresh install) → welcome screens with Flick. The text types itself with a soft vibration and no sound.
2. **Pick 2 pillars** (e.g. Body, Mind) → for each one, the app asks "What would winning look like?" with example chips.
3. **Type a goal for each pillar** (e.g. "Bench press 100kg", "Read 12 books") and pick a duration → Next works.
4. **Add a habit** → durations 5 / 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 / Custom, and it says "Works toward <your goal>".
5. **Close the app halfway, reopen it** → you continue where you were, with your goals still there.
6. **Sign the pledge** (hold the circle) → it lists your pillars and goals with the right dates.
7. Congratulations screen → **every goal** is mentioned with its date, plus a chip per pillar.
8. **Share my pledge** → a card in the app's style: "I'm taking back N years of my life.", your pledge with every goal, "Signed by <you>", Flick, "Make your own pledge →". Light/Dark, then the share sheet with the image and a short text with the link.

## B. Pillars & goals
9. **Goals tab → +** → create a pillar with a name, colour and icon (money icons are there; or type any emoji in "None fit?") → it appears in the list with that icon.
10. **Tap a pillar → Set a goal** → title, "done when", a date (a month grid; past days can't be picked) → the goal card shows progress, days left and pace.
11. **Set a second goal on the same pillar** → saved as **Next up**.
12. **Swipe a pillar left on the Goals tab → Archive** → it disappears, and its hours still count in Insights.
13. **A pillar with no goal** → no "Add habit"; it says "Set a goal first". After you set one, any habits it had move into the goal.
14. **Open a goal → Mark as achieved** → confetti, then a reflection prompt, then **Share this win** shows a trophy card.
15. **Drop a goal** → the sheet shows "Why you started" (if the pillar has a why) and the hours already given; if the goal has habits, choose **Move to <next goal>** / **Keep for the next goal** or **Stop them too**. It's gone from active goals, and the habits follow your choice.

## C. Tasks (steps of a goal; they live inside the goal, not on Today)
16. **In a goal, add 3 tasks** ("How long will it take?" 15 min / 30 min / 1 h…) and plans (Today / Tomorrow / Pick date) → each row shows "0 / 30 min · Today", and the first one says **Next**.
17. **Move a task up or down** → the **Next** label follows.
18. **Tap a task, edit it, Save** → the changes are saved.
19. **Delete a task** → it asks first, then it's gone (not marked done).
20. **Tick a task** → it moves to "1 done", and the next one becomes Next.

## D. Habits
21. **Add a habit** → no day is coloured until you tap one (or "Every day" / "Weekdays"); Add habit waits for a day. Pick a start time and Mon/Wed/Fri → on Today it only shows on those days.
22. ⏳ **At the habit's start time** → a notification with buttons (Start now / Snooze / Skip today).
23. **Tap "Start now" on the notification** → the Focus timer starts for that habit right away.
24. ⏳ **40+ minutes after a habit's start time** → Today shows "Missed 7:00 · 30 min" with **Do it now** / **Skip today**. Nothing is red.
25. **Skip today** → "Rest day · your streak is safe" with Undo, and "N of M done" doesn't count it.
26. **Swipe a habit left on Today** → Skip / Hide. **Long-press a habit** → a sheet with Skip today / Hide this habit.

## E. Focus sessions (the core)
27. **Focus tab** → per pillar, a goal card ("GOAL · 20 Gym sessions") with its **Next step** and its habits, then other habits, plus "Just give time to <pillar>". Finished tasks don't show.
28. **Start a task session, Pause, Resume, then End** → "Leave now?" sheet → **End session anyway** → "+12m to <goal>".
29. End screen → "Is this task done?" → **Yes** → "Task done".
30. **Free session from Today** (Free session → pick a pillar) → it runs and the time goes to that pillar.
31. **Let a short session finish on its own** → "Session complete", a success vibration, and the time added.
32. **Focus → Blocking card** → the Blocking screen: apps to block, "Every focus session · Always", **During my habits**, **Add block hours** (e.g. Weekdays 9:00–12:00). The card on Focus shows what you set. In Expo Go nothing is actually locked (it says so); in our own build the apps are locked at those times.

## F. Today
33. **Open Today** → greeting with your name, the **Flick stage pill** ("Spark · 10h to Flame"), the ring with minutes given today, "N of M done" (habits only). Per pillar: a **goal card** with the goal line ("3 steps ›") and **all** its habits; **no tasks**. Tapping the goal line opens the goal with its tasks. A pillar with habits but no goal shows them in a dashed "no goal running" card with **Set a goal**.
34. **Tap the stage pill** → opens **Your life in hours**.
35. **Do a session, then come back** → the ring and the minutes go up.
36. With several pillars → the pillar chips wrap (no sideways scroll), and pillars fold and unfold.

## G. Insights & your history
37. **Insights → WK / MO / ALL** → hours per pillar, the bars, and "X more than last week".
38. **"Flick is a Spark · 10h to Flame"** row → tap → Your life in hours.
39. **Your life in hours** → lifetime total, "since <date>", Flick's stage path, 12 months, pillars, goals achieved, **Milestones**.
40. **Your year so far** row → a story: total → where it went → best month → longest run → goals → how Flick grew → a card → **Share your year**.
41. **Habits this week** → a dot per day; a broken streak shows "Best: N days", never 0.

## H. Flick grows (the levels you liked)
42. Flick looks different per stage: Spark (small) → Flame (glow) → Torch (ember ring) → Bonfire → Beacon (crown of sparks) → Sun (rays).
43. ⏳ **Finish a session that takes your total past 10h (then 50h, 150h…)** → a full-screen "Flick is now a Flame. 10 hours given to the life you chose." with confetti and a strong vibration. **It never shows twice.**
44. A quiet week → Flick looks dimmer but never smaller and never loses its stage.

## I. Milestones
45. ⏳ **Pass 10h on one pillar** → "10 hours to Body." right after the session (after the stage-up if both happen, with **Next**).
46. **Share** on the milestone → a MILESTONE card with "10h" and the pillar.
47. Your life in hours → **Milestones** lists it with the date.
48. ⏳ A full week with every habit kept → "Every habit, kept all week."; 4 weeks with time on every pillar → "4 weeks in a row, every pillar got time."

## J. The week, your partner, Sunday
49. **Insights → Share your week** → a story card (hours, a bar per day, pillars, Flick), Light/Dark → share sheet.
50. **Choose an accountability partner** (Today "Who sees your week?" or Settings) → name and number → **send the invite** by Messages or the share sheet.
51. ⏳ **On Sunday (from noon) / Monday** → Today shows "Your week is ready" → **Open your week** → the Sunday ritual: hours → pillars → habits → goals → "make next week count" → **Send to <partner>** / **Plan next week**.
52. ⏳ **Sunday 6 PM** → the reminder notification (change the time in Settings → Sunday reminder).
53. **Weekly review** → "On track?" per goal, plan 1–3 tasks for next week → saved; reopening shows your answers.

## K. Settings & safety
54. **Replay onboarding** → your data is kept.
55. **Close and reopen the app** → everything is still there (pillars, goals, tasks, hours, milestones).
56. **Delete all data** (only if you want a clean slate) → it asks first, then you start fresh.
